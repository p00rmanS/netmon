How it works

A small box on your network does the watching. Nothing to install on your POS.

1

Checks every 30 seconds

NetMon checks every router, switch, Wi-Fi point, printer and tablet, and records how fast each one answers.

2

No false alarms

One missed check isn't an outage. A device is only marked down after three in a row, about 90 seconds.

3

Tells you what to do

Your manager's phone gets an alert with a plain-English fix, and another one when it's back.

Built for managers, not IT departments

One screen, red at the top

Every device on one page. Anything broken sorts to the top so it's impossible to miss.

Works on your phone

Check the dashboard from anywhere in the building. Dark mode for the late shift.

Alerts even when the internet is out

If the internet itself goes down, alerts are held and delivered the moment it's back.

A weekly report

Every Monday: outages, downtime and uptime for each device. Print it, or show it to your internet provider. See an example.

Your data stays on site

Speed charts

24-hour charts show when a device is getting slow, before it fails completely.
